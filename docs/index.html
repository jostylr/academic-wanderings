Exploratory

  • Following a Gaussian through five derivations Track a particle in a moving oscillator packet, release the trap, and compare two currents for spin.

  • Where the two Taylor polynomials part on a sphere Calculate a latitude’s Taylor jets two ways and vary the latitude and step to measure their difference.
  • One torus, two metrics Follow a parallel from a flat torus to a ring torus and watch the fourth-order correction change sign.
